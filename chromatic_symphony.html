Chromatic Symphony with QRNG

Welcome, Fellow Experimenter!

Hey there, artist! Imagine a place where every color you splash onto a canvas hums with its own tune, where reds growl low and violets sing high. That's "Chromatic Symphony," a digital playground made by Jamal Yusuf. This forked project takes the original project and adds a QRNG (Quantum Random Number Generator) element. Random numbers are used to generate the colours and tones.
